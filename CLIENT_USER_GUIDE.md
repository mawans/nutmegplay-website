# NutmegPlay Client User Guide

This guide is written for everyday users of NutmegPlay. It is intentionally non-technical and focuses on how to use the platform in real life.

Live website:

- `https://nutmegplay.fr`

## What NutmegPlay Is

NutmegPlay is a football platform for:

- logging in and managing your account
- viewing teams, matches, and player information
- uploading match videos
- turning those videos into AI-assisted player and match statistics
- reviewing player stats over time

Depending on your role, you may see different pages or options. Some users will only see player features, while coaches, instructors, or admins may see additional match and video tools.

## Before You Start

To use NutmegPlay smoothly, make sure you have:

- a working internet connection
- a modern browser such as Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox
- your login email and password
- a match video ready if you plan to upload one

If you do not yet have access, ask the project owner or administrator to create or activate your account.

## Signing In

1. Open `https://nutmegplay.fr`
2. Click `Log In`
3. Enter your email address
4. Enter your password
5. Click `Sign In`

If the login is successful, you will be taken into the platform.

If you click a menu item or page link, you will now see a thin progress bar at the top of the page. This is normal and simply confirms that your click has been received and the next page is loading.

## If You Forgot Your Password

1. Go to the login page
2. Click `Forgot password?`
3. Enter your email address
4. Follow the instructions sent to your email
5. Create a new password
6. Return to the website and sign in again

If the email does not arrive:

- check your spam or junk folder
- wait a few minutes
- make sure you entered the correct email address
- contact the project owner if the problem continues

## Match Video Upload: Full Tutorial

This is one of the most important parts of NutmegPlay.

The purpose of the `Video Upload` page is to:

- choose a match
- assign players to jersey numbers
- upload the match video
- let the AI process it
- store the results back into the platform

### Step 1: Open the Video Upload Page

Go to `Video Upload`.

You should see a list of available matches you are allowed to work with.

If you do not see the match you expect:

- refresh the page
- confirm you are logged into the correct account
- confirm the match exists in the system
- contact the administrator if it is still missing

### Step 2: Choose the Correct Match

Select the match you want to upload a video for.

Make sure it is the correct match before continuing.

This matters because the stats will be attached to that match and to the players assigned to it.

### Step 3: Assign Players to Jersey Numbers

Before uploading the video, make sure the lineup section is filled correctly.

NutmegPlay uses jersey slots to know which real player should receive which AI stats.

Current shirt-number setup:

- Blue team uses jerseys `1` to `5`
- Red team uses jerseys `6` to `10`

What you should do:

1. Review each jersey slot
2. Choose the correct player for that number
3. Make sure the blue side and red side are correct
4. Save the lineup if the page gives you that option

This step is very important.

If the lineup is wrong, the platform may still process the video, but the stored stats may be attached to the wrong player.

### Step 4: Upload the Video

Choose the match video file from your device and start the upload.

What happens now:

- you will see a real upload progress bar while the file is being sent
- the website stores the video safely first
- once the upload reaches 100%, the platform moves into AI processing

This is different from before. The system now gives a proper upload indicator instead of a vague “please wait” message.

### Step 4A: Make Sure the AI Engine Is On

If you are an instructor or administrator, you may also see an AI control panel on the upload page.

This panel is there to control the AI engine.

Possible states include:

- `Ready`
- `Starting`
- `Stopped`
- `Unavailable`

If the AI is not ready:

1. Click `Start AI`
2. Wait for the status to become ready
3. Then continue with the upload or let the queued video process

When you are finished with video work, an authorized user can click `Stop AI` to turn the processing engine off again.

This does not remove the already stored match video from the website.

### Step 5: Watch AI Processing Progress

After the upload finishes, the page continues showing AI progress.

You may see stages such as:

- `Preparing Job`
- `Analyzing Video`
- `Saving Results`
- `Processed`

This means:

- the system accepted the file
- the AI started working on it
- the results are being saved

Processing can take a few minutes depending on:

- video length
- server load
- amount of data being written back

Do not refresh repeatedly while it is running.

It is usually better to wait and let the status update naturally.

### Step 6: After Processing Completes

Once the match reaches a completed state:

- the processed result is saved
- match-level and player-level stats are written into the system
- the related player pages can reflect those updates

If the video is accepted but the results are not what you expect, check:

- whether the correct match was selected
- whether the lineup was filled correctly
- whether the video had a usable angle and enough visible play

## What Happens to Match Videos

For the current live setup, videos are stored on the NutmegPlay website and then processed by the AI engine.

From a user perspective, the important point is:

- upload the video
- allow processing to finish
- review the saved results afterward
- understand that old website-hosted videos are cleared automatically after the retention period

You do not need to manage the server or the AI system yourself.

## How Stats Are Stored and Used

In simple terms, the system works like this:

1. A match is selected
2. Players are tied to jersey numbers
3. The video is processed
4. The AI returns match and player information
5. NutmegPlay stores those results under the correct match
6. Player-facing stats are then shown in the platform

This is why the lineup selection step matters so much.

## What to Expect From AI Stats

The AI flow is working, but it is important to set expectations correctly.

You should treat the AI stats as:

- structured match analysis support
- useful performance information
- a tool to review and compare matches

You should not treat the first result of every single video as perfect without reviewing context.

As with any video-based system, output quality can depend on:

- video clarity
- camera angle
- lighting
- how much of the field is visible
- whether players can be followed consistently

## Best Practices for Better Results

For better video processing outcomes:

- use a clear and stable camera angle
- avoid very dark footage
- avoid heavily obstructed views
- upload the full usable match segment when possible
- make sure player assignments are correct before processing

## Common Questions

### “I clicked a page and nothing happened.”

You should now see a thin loading bar at the top when navigation starts. If the page is still taking time, wait a moment instead of clicking repeatedly.

### “My video upload finished. Why am I still waiting?”

Upload and AI processing are two separate stages.

The upload progress bar only covers the file transfer.
After that, the AI still needs time to analyze and save the results.

### “Why don’t I see a match?”

Possible reasons:

- you are logged into the wrong account
- the match has not been created yet
- the match is not assigned to your account
- you do not have permission for that match

### “Why are the stats attached to the wrong player?”

This usually means the jersey-to-player assignment was incorrect before processing.

### “Can I leave the page while processing?”

It is better to keep the page open until you see the final status. If you do leave, come back later and check whether the match has completed.

## Common Issues and What to Do

### Slow page loading

What to do:

- wait briefly for the first page load
- avoid repeated clicking
- refresh once only if truly stuck

Some pages naturally take longer because they gather more information.

### Upload problems

What to do:

- make sure your internet connection is stable
- wait for the upload bar to finish
- avoid closing the tab during upload

### Processing takes longer than expected

What to do:

- wait a few more minutes
- check whether the status changes from analysis to saving
- contact support if a match stays stuck for an unusually long time

### Login problems

What to do:

- confirm your email and password
- use `Forgot password?`
- contact the administrator if you still cannot access the site

## Recommended User Flow

For the smoothest day-to-day usage, follow this order:

1. Sign in
2. Go to the relevant match
3. Confirm the lineup and jersey assignments
4. Upload the video
5. Watch the upload progress bar
6. Wait for AI processing to complete
7. Review the player and match results
8. Return later to compare updated stats in the `Players` page
